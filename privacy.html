ProofPay Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ProofPay collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the ProofPay application and related services.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, username, and profile information.
  • Task descriptions and task-related content.
  • Task verification information.
  • Photos and videos submitted as task evidence.
  • Location information used for task verification.
  • Activity recognition information such as walking, running, movement state, and step counts.
  • Payment and transaction information.
  • Device and diagnostic information.

Location Data

ProofPay collects precise location information for task verification purposes. Depending on the task requirements, this may include:

  • GPS coordinates
  • Arrival and departure times
  • Route history
  • Dwell time at locations
  • Movement between locations

Location information may be collected while the application is in use and, when required for active task verification, while the application is running in the background.

Location information associated with a task may be visible to participants involved in the task, including the task sponsor and task performer, when necessary to verify task completion.

Activity Recognition Data

ProofPay may collect information related to physical activity, including walking activity, running activity, movement state, distance traveled, and step counts when required for task verification.

This information is used solely to determine whether task requirements have been satisfied and to help prevent fraud.

ProofPay is not a health, fitness, or medical application.

Photos and Videos

Users may upload photos and videos as evidence of task completion.

Uploaded media may be used for:

  • Task verification
  • Fraud prevention
  • Dispute resolution
  • Customer support
  • Security investigations

Photos and videos submitted as task evidence may be visible to the sponsor and performer associated with the task.

Authorized ProofPay administrators may access submitted media when necessary for verification review, fraud prevention, security investigations, dispute resolution, or customer support.

Task Evidence Visibility

Certain information submitted for task verification may be shared with participants in the task.

This may include:

  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Verification timestamps
  • Location history
  • Route information
  • Task completion evidence

Such information is shared solely for the purpose of verifying task completion and resolving disputes.

Maps and Location Services

ProofPay uses Google Maps Platform services to display maps, provide location-related functionality, and assist with location-based task verification.

Payment Processing

ProofPay uses Stripe and related payment services to process financial transactions.

Stripe may collect:

  • Payment card information
  • Billing information
  • Transaction information
  • Identity verification information when required

ProofPay does not store full payment card numbers.

Third-Party Services

ProofPay uses trusted third-party service providers to operate the platform.

  • Stripe for payment processing and financial transactions.
  • Google Maps Platform for mapping and geolocation services.
  • Supabase for authentication, database services, and secure storage of task evidence.
  • RevenueCat for subscription and entitlement management, if subscription services are offered.

How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Facilitate task creation and completion.
  • Verify task completion.
  • Process payments.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Investigate disputes.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Information Sharing

ProofPay may share information with:

  • Other participants involved in a task.
  • Payment processors.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers.
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers.
  • Government authorities when required by law.

ProofPay does not sell personal information.

Data Retention

Information may be retained as long as necessary to provide services, process payments, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Security

ProofPay uses commercially reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

  • Update account information.
  • Control device permissions.
  • Request account deletion.

Account Deletion

Information regarding account deletion is available on our Delete Account page.

Children

ProofPay is intended for users who are at least 18 years old.
