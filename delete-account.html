Delete Your ProofPay Account

Users may request deletion of their ProofPay account and associated personal information.

Requesting Account Deletion

To request account deletion, send an email to:

support@proofpay.app

Please send the request from the email address associated with your ProofPay account.

Data Removal

After account ownership is verified, ProofPay will process the deletion request.

Certain information may be retained where required for:

  • Fraud prevention
  • Dispute resolution
  • Security investigations
  • Financial recordkeeping
  • Legal compliance
